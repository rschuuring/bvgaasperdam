What to do about inappropriate behaviour

Inappropriate behaviour is a broad term. It is behaviour by other people that makes you feel uncomfortable or unsafe. Think of sexual harassment, aggressive behaviour or bullying.

At BV Gaasperdam we do not tolerate inappropriate behaviour around or during badminton at our club. Not from our own members, and not from members of other sports clubs, parents, supporters, trainers and/or passers-by in the sports hall either.

We have no indication of inappropriate behaviour within our club. Still, we want to pay attention to this subject on this page, because saying that you do not tolerate it is no guarantee that it will never happen.

Youth coaches

Youth coaches make sure our young members can play safely. To do so we follow the NOC*NSF code of conduct for coaches. It applies to everyone who supervises youth members.

  • We discuss the code of conduct with all youth coaches every year.
  • We ask all coaches for a certificate of conduct (VOG), renewed every four years.
  • For a new trainer we also ask other sports clubs for references.

The code of conduct in short

A coach:

  1. creates an environment and atmosphere in which players feel safe;
  2. treats players with respect and does not intrude on their private life further than necessary;
  3. does not abuse their position and refrains from any form of (sexual) intimidation;
  4. never has sexual contact or relationships with players under 16;
  5. does not touch players in a way that could be experienced as sexual or intimate;
  6. refrains from sexually charged verbal intimidation in any form;
  7. treats players with respect during training, matches and trips;
  8. protects players from harm and helps find a solution when something goes wrong;
  9. does not give or accept inappropriate rewards or gifts;
  10. addresses others whose behaviour breaks these rules and reports warning signs;
  11. acts in the spirit of these rules when a situation is not covered by them.

Breaking these rules can be dealt with through the disciplinary procedures of the sports federation. Have you seen something that is not right? Read what you can do and who our confidential advisor is.

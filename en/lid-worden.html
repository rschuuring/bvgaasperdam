Joining and cancelling

You can join two training sessions for free. When you decide to become a member, sign up with the registration form at the bottom of this page. You will then receive a confirmation from the membership administration.

Membership can only be cancelled per half year, in writing, by email only to ledenadministratie@bvgaasperdam.nl, at least one month in advance (so before 1 March or before 1 September). A cancellation is only valid once the membership administration has confirmed it in writing. If you cancel and sign up again within 1 year, a registration fee of € 30.00 is charged for administration costs.

Injuries

For injuries our club has a waiting period of 3 months. This means the first 3 months of an injury are at your own expense. After those 3 months you pay € 30.00 per half year as a non-playing member. This amount is settled afterwards with the next fee payment (in other words: no refunds or adjustments in between). The 3-month waiting period starts when the injury has been reported by email to ledenadministratie@bvgaasperdam.nl. You can take part in badminton activities again only after reporting your recovery to the membership administration.

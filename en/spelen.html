Join twice for free

Want to find out if badminton is for you? You can join two sessions for free, at a training or a free play evening. Send us an email beforehand so the hall supervisor knows you are coming.

info@bvgaasperdam.nl

The free sessions are an introduction and do not guarantee a place as a member.

What to bring

Indoor sports shoes that you don't wear outside, without black soles. Sports clothes and a water bottle. You can borrow a racket from us.

Where?

Sporthal Gaasperdam, Ravenswaaipad 5, Amsterdam Southeast. Navigate to Parkhof 100.

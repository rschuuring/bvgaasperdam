Welcome

Play badminton in Amsterdam Zuidoost

Want to find out if badminton is for you? You can join two sessions for free, at a training or a free play evening. Email us beforehand so the hall supervisor knows you are coming: info@bvgaasperdam.nl.

Bring indoor sports shoes that you don't wear outside, without black soles. You can borrow a racket.
